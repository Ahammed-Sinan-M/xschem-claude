Found while taking the first picture · issue 1379

The window says nothing has been sent to it, over the top of two things that were

The line above the pane reads “No device has been sent here yet” in every screenshot on this page — including the ones with two rendered blocks under it. rdw::_chrome_line takes that wording from llength $blocks, but the only proc that writes it into the widget is reached from rdw::build and rdw::set_list alone, and rdw::push calls neither. It repairs itself the moment you press 1, 2 or 3, which is why it survived.

This is issue 1367 in the mirror: that one claimed to be Showing an empty pane, and its fix minted the very flag that makes the two states sayable. The flag works; the recomputation was never wired. Filed, not fixed — this was a reporting batch.

Photographs taken on the persistent dev display (Xvfb 1920×1080×24, openbox 3.6.1) at commit a7cfa479, driving the shipped renderer — not a mock-up.

This box had no screenshot tool of any kind, so the batch built two: tests/headless/winshot.c grabs any X window to a PNG, and tests/headless/lookshot.sh poses xschem, waits for the window, takes the picture and kills it. The driver takes the picture rather than the pose script, which is what lets a modal be captured at all.
